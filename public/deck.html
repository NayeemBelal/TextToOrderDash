✦ Gamified text marketing for restaurants

Turn your customer list into a community that plays.

Belan texts each of your customers a quick game. They reply, they win, and they come in before the prize runs out. Every winner brings a friend. Every round makes the list bigger.

The problem

Discounts are given away. Nobody earns them, so nobody moves.

“10% off” is wallpaper

Every restaurant sends the same blast. It asks for nothing, so it gets nothing. After a few, your number is noise.

A coupon with no clock sits in a drawer

The list never grows

You text the same 300 people forever. Nobody on it has a reason to bring anyone else in.

Belan Marketing AI04
Why a game beats a coupon

People ignore offers. They don’t ignore a question.

Reply

A text that asks gets answered

A blast is a one-way notice. A game asks one small thing, and answering it takes less effort than deleting it. That reply is the moment a blast never gets.

Earn

Won beats given

A prize you played for feels like yours. People value what they earned far above what was handed to them, and they don’t let it go to waste.

Repeat

A ritual, not a promo

Trivia Tuesday, Dice Friday. Regulars start expecting it, talking about it, and playing it together. That’s a community forming around your restaurant.

Every reply is graded automatically. Winners get the prize, everyone else can get a consolation offer, and nobody is left with nothing to do next.

Belan Marketing AI07
An ever-growing list

Three doors in, all of them consented. None of them out.

Refer

Winners bring friends

On every coupon page: “Invite a friend, get +5%.” The friend gets a welcome offer by text, the referrer’s coupon grows once it’s delivered. Your customers recruit for you.

Scan

QR at the counter

A card on the counter, the tables and the receipt. Scan, enter a number, tick consent, get a welcome coupon. Every walk-in becomes a member you can reach.

Import

The list you already have

Loyalty sign-ups, online ordering opt-ins, a spreadsheet from the counter. Drop in an Excel file and they’re in the next game.

Each round of a game ends with more people on the list than it started with. The people who join through a friend are the best members you’ll ever have: they came because someone they trust won something here.
